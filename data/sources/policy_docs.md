# Commercial Property Insurance Policy
## Policy Reference: CP-2024-EURO-001
## Insurer: EuroShield Insurance Group
## Policyholder: Meridian Commercial Properties B.V.

---

## Coverage Clauses

### Clause 1 — Water Damage Coverage
The insurer covers sudden and accidental water damage caused by the failure
of fixed plumbing installations, including burst pipes, valve failures, and
cistern overflows. Coverage applies provided the damage is reported within
**72 hours** of discovery and the estimated repair cost exceeds **€5,000**.
Claims below this threshold are not eligible for reimbursement.

### Clause 2 — Weather-Related Structural Damage
The insurer covers structural damage directly caused by extreme weather
events, defined as wind speeds exceeding **90 km/h** or precipitation
exceeding **40mm within a 24-hour period** at the incident location. The
reference for wind speed and precipitation levels should come from a certified weather
station within **50km** of the property. Damage attributable to gradual
weathering, poor maintenance, or pre-existing structural weakness is
excluded regardless of weather conditions at the time of the incident.

### Clause 3 — Consequential Loss Following Contractor Work
The insurer covers water or structural damage occurring within **30 days**
of completion of any licensed contractor work on plumbing, roofing, or
load-bearing structures, provided the damage can be **reasonably attributed**
to the contractor work. Where the causal link between the contractor work
and the damage is disputed, the insurer may commission an independent
technical assessment. Coverage under this clause is subject to the
policyholder retaining all contractor invoices and work completion
certificates. Whether damage that manifests gradually over the 30-day
period qualifies as a single covered event or multiple separate incidents
is determined **at the insurer's discretion**.

### Clause 4 — Exclusion: Negligence and Deferred Maintenance
The insurer **explicitly excludes** all claims arising from the
policyholder's failure to carry out routine maintenance as defined in the
policy schedule. This includes but is not limited to: known plumbing
defects left unrepaired for more than **90 days**, roof deterioration
documented in prior inspection reports, and structural issues flagged by
municipal authorities. Claims will be denied where the insurer can
demonstrate that the damage was foreseeable and preventable through
reasonable maintenance practices. Prior knowledge of a defect by the
policyholder's facilities management team constitutes grounds for
exclusion regardless of whether a formal repair order was raised.

### Clause 5 — Repeat Claims Condition
Where a policyholder submits **two or more claims of the same damage type**
within a **36-month rolling period**, the insurer reserves the right to
apply an additional excess of **20% of the claim value** on the second and
subsequent claims. The insurer further reserves the right to commission a
structural survey at the policyholder's expense before processing any third
claim of the same type within the same period. This clause applies per
property address and is not transferable across portfolio properties.

### Clause 6 — Repair Estimate Verification
For all claims exceeding **€10,000**, the insurer requires submission of a
detailed repair estimate from a licensed contractor before processing the
claim. The estimate must itemise all labour, materials, and ancillary costs
separately. Where the submitted repair estimate is unavailable or incomplete,
the insurer reserves the right to appoint an independent loss adjuster at
the policyholder's expense.

For claims between **€5,000 and €10,000**, submission of a repair estimate
is recommended but not mandatory. The insurer may request one at its
discretion if the reported damage scope is inconsistent with the claimed
amount.

Claims below **€5,000** are not eligible for reimbursement under Clause 1
and do not require a repair estimate.
